About

I am a programmer and visiting lecturer at HSE University’s School of Philological Studies, Faculty of Humanities. I develop research software and teach on the Literature and Digital Humanities course.

Together with Evgeny Kazartsev, I work on interpretable stylometric methods. I also contribute to SOCIOLIT and PROZIMETRON and write practical guides to computational text analysis.

Research platform

SOCIOLIT

Explore SOCIOLIT ↗

Tools for studying Russian literature and its relationship with society.

SOCIOLIT is a project of HSE University’s School of Philological Studies. It brings together texts and tools for exploring word use, comparing corpora, and analysing sentiment through a web interface.

  • Search for words and lemmas in literary corpora
  • Compare word distributions across groups of texts
  • Explore word frequencies and the emotional tone of a text

As a core developer, I work on analytical modules, corpus processing, APIs, and interfaces for researchers.
